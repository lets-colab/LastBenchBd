# Last Bench — Product Constitution

## Current category

**Student Accelerator · technology-enabled journey platform.**

Last Bench is not positioned as a traditional education consultancy. The current commercial entry point is student mobility from **Bangladesh → Malaysia**. The public promise is:

> **Helping Bangladeshi students study, settle and succeed in Malaysia.**

The wider blueprint is one trusted relationship with three engines:

- **Last Bench — trust / mobility:** direction, university selection, admissions, visa guidance, pre-departure, arrival and community.
- **CLASS[Λ] — capability:** AI, content, branding, attention and proof-of-work.
- **co.lab — ownership:** venture validation, automation and market launch.

Do not present later wings as if they are already the reason a student should buy the current mobility service. Mobility must stand on its own outcomes and trust.

## Established now vs blueprint next

### Established public mobility promise

- Malaysia university selection based on profile, course, budget and intake.
- Admissions/application support and required documentation.
- Visa/EMGS process guidance and document preparation.
- Scholarship / rebate / funding-route verification where currently available.
- Pre-departure preparation.
- Practical settlement and community support after arrival.

### Productizing now

The **Journey OS** turns the service into a visible process. Students, families, counselors and operations should converge on the same next step, owner, status and evidence.

### Progression wings

CLASS[Λ] and co.lab belong to the longer human lifecycle, but they are progression pathways rather than homepage acquisition claims for the mobility business. They should be promoted only when the relevant offer/pilot is active, capacity exists and the handoff is real.

## Core public journey

The mobility website and Journey OS should use one six-stage path:

1. **DISCOVER** — goals, profile, budget and preferred intake.
2. **MATCH** — realistic courses and institutions.
3. **APPLY** — documents, application and offer.
4. **SECURE** — visa, payments and financial readiness.
5. **PREPARE** — travel, documentation and pre-departure.
6. **ARRIVE** — settlement, community and first practical steps.

Operating rule: **every person has a current stage, next milestone, accountable owner and verified proof/evidence.**

## Beyond arrival

The relationship should not reset after the flight lands:

**SETTLE → BELONG → PROGRESS → RETURN**

Community after arrival is part of the current promise. Capability, venture-building, alumni stories, referrals and mentoring extend lifetime value only after the underlying student outcome is real.

## Audiences and entry points

### Student / parent

Primary public question: **“Help me choose and move with clarity.”**

Primary CTA: **Start my journey.**
Secondary CTA for registered users: **Open Journey OS / track my journey.**

### Tutor / coaching / IELTS / education partner

Secondary public pathway: **“Help us guide and develop students.”**

Partner onboarding and commercial terms must be confirmed through the operating team before anyone represents offers, commissions or university relationships publicly.

### Creator / builder

These belong to CLASS[Λ] and co.lab respectively and should not compete with the mobility conversion path on the main Last Bench homepage while those wings are not the active acquisition priority.

## Website logic

The homepage must do this in order:

1. Make the Bangladesh → Malaysia journey emotionally real.
2. Explain what Last Bench actually does today.
3. Show the six visible journey milestones.
4. Let students explore university options without implying every listed university is a current partner.
5. Convert a qualified student into a lead or registered Journey OS user.
6. Show that support continues after arrival.
7. Offer a quiet secondary partner pathway without turning the student homepage into an agent-recruitment page.

The website must **not** lead with CLASS[Λ], co.lab, speculative future platform features or unverified incentives while the current commercial promise is mobility.

## Trust rules

- Student first.
- Transparency over persuasion.
- Outcomes over admissions.
- Community before consultancy.
- Technology serves humans.
- No guaranteed visa outcome.
- No fixed scholarship/rebate percentage without a current verified source.
- No fabricated admissions, fee, intake, acceptance, visa-success or partner data.
- A university listed for exploration is not automatically a claim of a Last Bench partnership.
- High-stakes AI guidance escalates to a human and identifies what requires official verification.

## Brand personality

Cinematic, sincere, determined. **“From the last bench. To the world.”** The underdog framing is pride and forward motion, never pity.

Visual law: dark for emotion, white for trust, green for progress.

## North-star product logic

Do not optimize the platform around page views or application volume alone. Optimize for **qualified people reaching the next meaningful stage with verified evidence, accountable ownership and improving economic quality.**
