A browser-based recruitment decision-support MVP that converts a vacancy into a Job DNA, reads candidate text, scores only against job evidence, explains the result and lets HR export a shortlist. Candidate data stays in the browser.

BEFORE → AFTER

Before: manual screening

  • Open CVs one by one.
  • Hold job requirements in human memory.
  • Repeated comparisons are slow and inconsistent.
  • Evidence for a shortlist is hard to audit.
→

After: structured decision support

  • Define the Job DNA once.
  • Normalize candidate text.
  • Score visible evidence against requirements.
  • HR sees reasons, gaps and evidence strength before deciding.

Governance boundary

This tool is decision support, not an automatic hiring system. Protected-characteristic screening terms are blocked from the Job DNA in this MVP, and the tool does not make final employment decisions. HR must still review source evidence, validate job relevance, follow applicable employment law and apply company policy before progressing a candidate.
