From concept to commerce / 004

Trust is the experience.

The system will never claim a product is in stock based on a photo.

01

Verify the exact item

Read barcode, volume, formulation, shade, batch requirements and real packaging. Distinguish similar variants.

Photo evidence → SKU confirmation
02

Confirm the business truth

Proprietor validates price, availability, provenance and whether the product may be offered online.

Owner approval → sellable record
03

Switch on the engine

Only then connect stock-aware ordering, payments, courier tracking, loyalty and operational reporting.

Testing → launch → verified fulfillment
