# Last Bench — Logo Usage Standard

Status: **hard identity rule**

## Source of truth

Master/reference board:

`reference/logo-lockups.png`

Machine-readable enforcement:

`brand-lock.json`

## Mandatory background rule

The Last Bench logo must always be used with a **transparent background**.

### On light / white / warm-white backgrounds

Use:

- black/charcoal `LAST BENCH` wordmark;
- Brand Green bench + rising arrow;
- black/charcoal base tagline text;
- Brand Green `BENCHMARK`.

### On dark / black / charcoal backgrounds

Use:

- white `LAST BENCH` wordmark;
- Brand Green bench + rising arrow;
- white base tagline text;
- Brand Green `BENCHMARK`.

## Never

- bake a black rectangle into the logo;
- bake a white rectangle into the logo;
- add a logo card/plate merely for contrast;
- use white wordmark on a light background;
- use black wordmark on a dark background;
- recolor the green bench-arrow to white;
- redraw the logo;
- recreate the wordmark with a font;
- regenerate the logo with AI;
- stretch, skew, rotate or change proportions.

If contrast is insufficient, move the logo to a suitable area or adjust the surrounding composition.

## Icon

The standalone bench + rising-arrow icon remains Brand Green.

Use it only when the full wordmark is unnecessary or space is intentionally constrained. It is not a replacement for the full logo in primary brand-signature positions.

## Clear space

Keep enough clear space around the logo so no text, image edge, partner logo or CTA visually collides with it.

## Partner lockups

Do not fuse the Last Bench logo with a university or partner logo into a new hybrid mark.

Keep identities separate with deliberate spacing and hierarchy.

## AI / generative workflow

AI may generate only the unbranded background, photography or scene.

The exact logo is composited after generation.
